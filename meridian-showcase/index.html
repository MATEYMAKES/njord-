Perspective

The view from higher ground

Markets move constantly. Mountains move slowly. We pay attention to both.

Short-term movement looks like noise until it is seen in context. The wider the view, the clearer the route. Our work begins by understanding conditions as they are. Only then do we commit capital to a direction.

Approach

A disciplined ascent

Long-term growth is rarely a straight line. It is a route, planned in advance and walked with care.

Stage I

Survey

We study the terrain: market conditions, risk, and the objectives of the capital entrusted to us.

Stage II

Route

We set a long-term direction and define the limits we will hold to along the way.

Stage III

Ascent

We move deliberately, review continuously, and adjust when conditions require it. Sentiment alone is not a reason to change course.

Principles

What holds us to the route

Conditions change. The principles that guide our decisions do not.

  • Long horizons

    We measure progress in years and decades. Short-term movement informs our view. It does not set our course.

  • Measured risk

    Every position is taken with a clear understanding of what could go wrong and how much we are prepared to accept.

  • Independent perspective

    We form our own view from evidence, and we are prepared to hold it when it is unpopular.

  • Patience

    Discipline includes the decision not to act. We do not trade to appear busy.

  • Clear communication

    Clients should always know where they stand, what we are doing, and why.
